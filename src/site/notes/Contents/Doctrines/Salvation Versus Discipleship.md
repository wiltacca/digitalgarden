# Salvation & Discipleship Distinguished, But Not Separated

## Salvation Is God's Free Gift

**Salvation is received by grace through faith.**

> “For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God:  
> Not of works, lest any man should boast.”  
> **Ephesians 2:8–9**

**Salvation is based entirely upon the finished work of Jesus Christ.**

> “For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.”  
> **John 3:16**

**The believer is justified—declared righteous before God—through faith.**

> “Therefore being justified by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ:”  
> **Romans 5:1**

**The believer possesses everlasting life and is no longer under condemnation.**

> “Verily, verily, I say unto you, He that heareth my word, and believeth on him that sent me, hath everlasting life, and shall not come into condemnation; but is passed from death unto life.”  
> **John 5:24**

---
## Discipleship Is Following Christ

**A disciple continues faithfully in the Word of Christ.**

> “Then said Jesus to those Jews which believed on him, If ye continue in my word, then are ye my disciples indeed;”  
> **John 8:31**

**Discipleship requires daily self-denial and submission to Christ.**

> “And he said to them all, If any man will come after me, let him deny himself, and take up his cross daily, and follow me.”  
> **Luke 9:23**

**A disciple follows Christ’s example by serving others.**

> “For even the Son of man came not to be ministered unto, but to minister, and to give his life a ransom for many.”  
> **Mark 10:45**

**A disciple grows spiritually and becomes increasingly like Christ.**

> “But we all, with open face beholding as in a glass the glory of the Lord, are changed into the same image from glory to glory, even as by the Spirit of the Lord.”  
> **2 Corinthians 3:18**

---
# The Proper Relationship

## Saved By Grace Through Faith

Salvation is not earned through obedience, personal sacrifice, baptism, church attendance, religious service, or good works.

> “For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God:  
> Not of works, lest any man should boast.”  
> **Ephesians 2:8–9**

## Created Unto Good Works

Good works are not the cause of salvation. They are the purpose and expected fruit of the new life God gives.

> “For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus unto good works, which God hath before ordained that we should walk in them.”  
> **Ephesians 2:10**

## Saved According To God's Mercy

Salvation does not come through righteous deeds performed by the sinner.

> “Not by works of righteousness which we have done, but according to his mercy he saved us, by the washing of regeneration, and renewing of the Holy Ghost;”  
> **Titus 3:5**

---
## True Faith Is Living Faith

Good works do not purchase salvation, but genuine saving faith does not remain fruitless.

> “Even so faith, if it hath not works, is dead, being alone.”  
> **James 2:17**

James is not teaching that works earn eternal life. He is teaching that genuine faith produces visible evidence in the believer’s life.

---
## Biblical Balance

Salvation and discipleship must be carefully distinguished, but they should never be completely separated.

**Salvation** is God’s free gift, received by grace through faith in Jesus Christ.

**Discipleship** is the believer’s continuing life of learning, obedience, service, sacrifice, and spiritual growth.

We are not saved **by good works**, but we are saved **unto good works**.

## Not By Works, But Unto Good Works

- Saved by His grace.  
- Saved through faith.  
- Saved to follow Christ.  
- Saved to serve Him.  
- Saved to glorify Him.

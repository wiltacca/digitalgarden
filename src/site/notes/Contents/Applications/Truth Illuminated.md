>[!NOTE]+ QUOTE
“We need to learn that truth consists not in correct doctrine, but in correct doctrine plus the inward enlightenment of the Holy Spirit. This vital truth could result in a fresh breath from God upon a stale and suffocating orthodoxy.” – A.W. Tozer

# The Source: A.W. Tozer

Tozer served as a pastor for 44 years, most prominently at Southside Alliance Church in Chicago (1928–1959), where his preaching drew students from Moody Bible Institute, Wheaton College, and the University of Chicago.

Tozer’s statement contains an important warning, though it should be expressed with careful biblical balance. 

Correct doctrine is indispensable. Scripture repeatedly commands believers to hold “sound doctrine” and to reject error:

> “But speak thou the things which become sound doctrine.”  
> Titus 2:1

> “Take heed unto thyself, and unto the doctrine; continue in them.”  
> 1 Timothy 4:16

Yet a person may possess accurate theological knowledge while remaining spiritually cold, proud, or unchanged. The Lord Jesus said to certain religious men:

> “Ye search the scriptures; for in them ye think ye have eternal life: and they are they which testify of me. And ye will not come to me, that ye might have life.”  
> John 5:39–40

Their problem was not that Scripture was insufficient, but that they studied it without truly coming to Christ. They had information, but not spiritual life.

---
# The Holy Spirit’s Illumination

The inward enlightenment of the Holy Spirit does not create new truth or correct the Bible. Rather, the Spirit enables believers to understand, receive, and obey the truth already revealed in Scripture.

> “But the natural man receiveth not the things of the Spirit of God: for they are foolishness unto him: neither can he know them, because they are spiritually discerned.”  
> 1 Corinthians 2:14

> “Open thou mine eyes, that I may behold wondrous things out of thy law.”  
> Psalm 119:18

> “Howbeit when he, the Spirit of truth, is come, he will guide you into all truth.”  
> John 16:13

The Spirit of God opens the understanding, convicts the conscience, reveals the glory of Christ, and applies the Word personally. He does not lead contrary to Scripture, for He is the Author of Scripture:

> “Holy men of God spake as they were moved by the Holy Ghost.”  
> 2 Peter 1:21

Therefore, any claimed “inward enlightenment” that contradicts the written Word cannot be attributed to the Holy Spirit.

---
# Dead Orthodoxy

Tozer’s phrase “stale and suffocating orthodoxy” describes a condition in which biblical truths are confessed outwardly but have ceased to affect the heart. Scripture gives several examples of this danger.

The church at Ephesus was doctrinally discerning and morally vigilant, yet Christ said:

> “Nevertheless I have somewhat against thee, because thou hast left thy first love.”  
> Revelation 2:4

The church at Sardis had a reputation for life, but Christ declared:

> “Thou hast a name that thou livest, and art dead.”  
> Revelation 3:1

Paul also warned of those:

> “Having a form of godliness, but denying the power thereof.”  
> 2 Timothy 3:5

These passages show that outward correctness, religious reputation, and doctrinal form can exist without spiritual vitality.

---
# Doctrine & Spiritual Life Must Remain United

The biblical answer is not to choose spiritual experience instead of doctrine. Nor is it to choose doctrine without spiritual experience. The Word and the Spirit must never be separated.

Truth must be:

- Believed by the mind,

- Received in the heart,

- Applied by the Holy Spirit,

- And demonstrated through obedience.

Paul prayed that believers would receive spiritual understanding:

> “That the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of glory, may give unto you the spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of him: the eyes of your understanding being enlightened.”  
> Ephesians 1:17–18

He also prayed that they would be:

> “Filled with the knowledge of his will in all wisdom and spiritual understanding; that ye might walk worthy of the Lord unto all pleasing.”  
> Colossians 1:9–10

True enlightenment therefore produces holiness, humility, love for Christ, and obedience to Scripture. It is not merely an emotional sensation.

---
# Conclusion

Tozer’s central concern is sound: correct doctrine alone, when held merely intellectually, can become lifeless orthodoxy. However, truth itself remains objective and is revealed in God’s Word. The Holy Spirit does not add to biblical truth; He illuminates it, makes it effectual in the heart, and transforms the believer through it.

The proper prayer is therefore not for doctrine to be replaced by spiritual experience, but for sound doctrine to be made living and powerful through the gracious ministry of the Holy Ghost:

> “Quicken thou me according to thy word.”  
> Psalm 119:25, KJV
